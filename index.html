About Nkothi Projects

A partner in success.

From construction sites to office floors, from farms to medical facilities — we deliver solutions across every sector we serve.

Nkothi Projects (Pty) Ltd is a 100% South African multi-disciplinary company headquartered in Mahikeng. We operate across a broad spectrum of industries — construction, infrastructure development, equipment supply, cleaning, catering, travel, and more.

Our approach is built on three things: satisfaction, professionalism, and outstanding outcomes. Whether you're a government department, a private business, or an individual client, we bring the same level of care and capability to every project.
